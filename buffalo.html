Canisius is the most painful row in this pass

Division 1 in the MAAC, 3.3 miles from downtown, and on the running one of the best fits anywhere in this sweep: across three championships he arrives as their 5th man, 86 seconds behind their #1 and 48 seconds inside their seventh, into a seven that spans 134 seconds. Then the sponsorship check: its athletics site publishes a single combined cross country section and not one track page, for either gender.

So it sits on the no-track table below rather than at the top of the list. It is the first row on this whole board to reopen if running cross country only ever becomes acceptable.

One of the six rows is unmeasured, and one is measured thin

Bryant & Stratton is the unmeasured one: a USCAA college rather than an NCAA or NAIA one, 0.6 miles from the centre, and the entire men's cross country record on file is two men in one 2026 invitational. At $14,135 it is the cheapest row on the page, and the federal file reports no admit rate and no SAT range for it at all.

Hilbert is the other, and it is a different thing: it finished two men at the 2025 AMCC championship and two at the Division 3 Niagara regional. A conference championship is the race a program brings everyone to, so two men there is a measurement of its depth rather than a gap in the evidence — he would arrive 209 seconds ahead of their #1 at that championship and 106 ahead at the regional, which is why this row reads Caution and not Verify. Its athletics site is a further worry: the navigation lists neither cross country nor track among its sports, and the most recent schedules it publishes are fall 2025 for cross country and spring 2025 for men's outdoor track. Confirm both sports still exist before spending time on it.

What is not on this page yet

Every tier on this page comes from the published ladder run over 2025 championship results — conference, regional and national meets only. Three things the older metro pages have and this one does not, yet: no track marks, so there is no 5000m gap chart and no 1500 field here; no coach names, emails or Instagram handles; and no meet maps. The one screen that is complete is the both-sports rule — men's outdoor track sponsorship was checked school by school against the sport list each athletics site publishes itself, because TFRRS cannot answer it.

The ones worth real effort

  1. University at Buffalo — D1 Mid-American, 11.1 miles, 7.9% CS, 74% admit, $41,595 on his residency as a New York public, and he lands two seconds inside their seventh man, 82 behind their #1. The only target-tier row in the metro and the only Division 1 program in it. Its price is also the highest here by $19,000, so the non-resident waiver question is the first one to ask.
  2. Buffalo State — D3 SUNYAC, 5.1 miles, 5.4% CS, $22,466, and he would arrive 35 seconds ahead of their #1 and 261 inside their seventh. Caution tier, cheap, and central; a place to be the team rather than join one.
  3. Daemen and D'Youville — the two Division 2 programs and therefore the only partial-athletic-money doors in range, at $18,693 and $20,433. Both are thin: he would lead Daemen by 16 seconds and D'Youville by 137, and D'Youville never finished seven runners.
