## 5. Top picks

1. **Fordham** — the best overall fit in the metro. Atlantic 10 D1, real CS department, a clean ~25–35s gap to their #1, and they train at **Van Cortlandt Park**, the most historic cross country course in America, right next to campus. Solves the "where do you actually run in New York" problem better than anyone.
2. **Hofstra** — Coastal Athletic D1, ~40s gap, Long Island campus with actual space to train, ~70% acceptance. Clean athletic and academic fit.
3. **Wagner** — Staten Island, ~40s gap, easy admission, D1. Underrated.
4. **Seton Hall** — Big East, ~25–35s gap. Their distance squad is weaker than the conference name suggests, which works in his favor.
5. **Stevens Institute of Technology** — if CS leads the decision. One of the strongest CS/engineering schools on this list, Hoboken, D3, and no athletic-scholarship pressure. **Get their roster times before committing time here.**
6. **NJIT** — D1 with a genuine tech-school CS program at ~65% acceptance. Best D1-plus-CS value in the metro if the running fits; needs roster data.

## 6. NYC-specific considerations

**Cost of living is the dominant variable.** Fordham, NYU, and Stevens are among the most expensive schools in the country once housing is included, and NYC-area housing costs will swamp a partial athletic scholarship. The public options — Montclair State, Kean, William Paterson, Ramapo, the CUNYs, Queens College — change the math entirely. **Model total cost of attendance including housing, not tuition.**

**Training terrain is a genuine question to raise with coaches.** Ask specifically where the team does long runs and workouts. Good answers: Van Cortlandt Park (Fordham, Manhattan, Iona), Central Park, the Rockefeller State Park carriage trails, Bear Mountain for hills, Long Island's parks (Hofstra). Bad answer: a hesitation, or "we do a lot on the track."

**Three states, three sets of in-state tuition.** NJ publics (Montclair, Kean, Ramapo, William Paterson, NJIT, Rutgers) and NY publics (CUNYs, Queens College, Purchase, Farmingdale) will charge out-of-state rates coming from South Carolina. Factor that in before ranking them on price.
