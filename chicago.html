The 20-mile radius is the binding constraint

Inside the radius there are four D1 options, and most of the D3 list consists of programs where he would be the #1 runner on day one — the bad outcome, not the good one. The programs that would develop him sit at 25 to 30 miles.

Recommendation: widen this to 30 miles. It costs about fifteen minutes of driving and it adds Lewis University, which on the available evidence is a better athletic-and-CS fit than anything inside 20 miles. If the radius is fixed for a reason, the list below stands — but it is thin, and that is worth knowing going in.

The ones worth real effort

  1. DePaul — 3 miles from the Loop, Big East D1, a genuinely strong CS program in its School of Computing, and a clean 55–65 second gap to their #1. An 8:23 3000 and a 29:52 10K confirm a functioning distance squad rather than one fast athlete. Best fit inside the radius, comfortably.
  2. Loyola Chicago — 8 miles, Atlantic 10, ~80% acceptance, the same 55–65 second gap, lakefront campus with real training access. Essentially co-equal with DePaul; choose on CS department fit and money.
  3. Lewis University (30 mi) — worth breaking the radius for. D2 means actual scholarship dollars, the distance program is strong, and it has a CS degree.
  4. UIC — the cheapest strong option with a good CS department, but resolve the depth question first. Their 13:56 best and 28:56 10K are a strong top end; one fast senior is not depth.
  5. University of Chicago — only if his academic profile supports a ~5% admit rate. Elite CS, good conference (the same UAA as NYU and Emory). Currently a complete blank on times, and that is the first thing to fix.
The hard truth about Illinois Tech

It has one of the best CS programs on this list and it is 4 miles from downtown — but its distance squad is running 17:46 for the 5000. He would beat their best runner by two and a half minutes. That is not a training environment; it is a solo career. If CS leads the decision and he accepts that, fine — but go in knowing it, and ask the coach directly what they are recruiting.

Three things specific to Chicago

Illinois public tuition. UIC and Chicago State are excellent value for Illinois residents and much less so from South Carolina. Confirm out-of-state rates before ranking either on price.

Winter training is a real factor. The indoor season runs through genuine Chicago winter, and lakefront wind is not a joke. Ask what indoor facility each program has access to — a full 200m banked track versus a fieldhouse straightaway meaningfully changes indoor development. DePaul, Loyola, and UIC all have different arrangements; ask specifically.

Training terrain. The lakefront path is excellent for volume and flat as a table. Ask where they find hills; the answer is usually Waterfall Glen, Palos, or Busse Woods, all a drive away. A program that never runs hills will limit him as a 5K and cross country runner.
