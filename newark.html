Why this page exists when New York already carried these rows

Twelve of these thirteen rows are inside 20 driving miles of downtown Newark and carried on the New York page — the overlap is deliberate. What changes is the number in front of them. Rutgers–Newark reads as 13 miles from Midtown and half a mile from downtown Newark; Kean reads as 20 and 6.9; NJIT as 12 and 0.8. A page anchored on Midtown ranks them as outliers, and a page anchored on Newark ranks them as a cluster.

Each row calls home whichever centre is actually nearer, which is what the Overview and its own school page name. Three of the twelve — Saint Peter's, Stevens Institute and Fairleigh Dickinson — are nearer Midtown and call New York home; the rest call Newark home. Drew at 16.2 miles is on this page only.

NJIT and Seton Hall

NJIT is the strongest computer science row on the entire board by share of degrees — 33% — and it is not a trade-off row: Division 1 in America East, 65% admit, $34,194 on his residency as a New Jersey public, and on the running their 3rd or 4th man, 125 seconds inside their scoring seven and 26 behind their #1. The one caution is that a 125-second margin inside a seven means their back end is soft, so read it as "he would score immediately", not "he would be pushed".

Seton Hall is the tighter squad: Big East Division 1, 4.2 miles, 73% admit, $31,446, and he lands as their 4th or 5th man, 47 seconds inside their seven and 44 behind their #1. A 2.4% CS share is the reason to ask hard questions about the department.

Three New Jersey publics in range sponsor no men’s cross country

NJCU (3.3 miles), Montclair State (13.6) and William Paterson (19.5) are all NJAC members and none of them fields a men's cross country team: NJCU and William Paterson sponsor women's cross country and men's outdoor track, Montclair State sponsors men's track and not cross country. That is three of the conference's local footprint gone, and it is why the NJAC rows that do exist here — Rutgers–Newark and Kean — are so thin.

What is not on this page yet

Every tier on this page comes from the published ladder run over 2025 championship results — conference, regional and national meets only. Three things the older metro pages have and this one does not, yet: no track marks, so there is no 5000m gap chart and no 1500 field here; no coach names, emails or Instagram handles; and no meet maps. The one screen that is complete is the both-sports rule — men's outdoor track sponsorship was checked school by school against the sport list each athletics site publishes itself, because TFRRS cannot answer it.

The ones worth real effort

  1. NJIT — D1 America East, 0.8 miles from downtown, 33% CS — the highest share on the board — 65% admit, $34,194 on his residency, and their 3rd or 4th man. If one row in the New York area is worth a first email, this is it.
  2. Seton Hall — D1 Big East, 4.2 miles, 73% admit, $31,446, their 4th or 5th man and 47 seconds inside their seven. The better squad of the two targets; the weaker computer science department at 2.4%.
  3. Stevens Institute — D3 MAC, 21.2% CS, 48% admit, and $41,346, the most expensive row on the page. Caution tier: he would arrive 45 seconds ahead of their #1. It calls New York home at 3 miles from Midtown, and it is on this page because Hoboken is 10 miles from Newark.

And the money row. Saint Peter's is Division 1 in the MAAC at $12,199 — the cheapest row anywhere in the New York area and a third of what Stevens costs — with a 90% admit rate and 6.9% CS. He would arrive 31 seconds ahead of their #1, so it is caution tier, but Division 1 money at that price is worth an email regardless of the tier.
